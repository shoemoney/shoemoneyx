# Landing page thumbnails

Actual screenshots of all eighteen ShoeMoneyX landing designs, captured locally through computer use with `?demo=1`. All financial values in these images are simulated.

Refreshed on September 6, 2026 after the Picaso company-logo rollout and final typography/black-background polish. Each image shows the supplied original logo integrated into its own visual treatment. Prism and Spectrum now use dark surfaces; every wordmark X is blue. The catalog uses `?v=signature-polish` to invalidate earlier previews.

Captured at 1440 × 960 and saved as 960 × 640 JPEGs. The gallery crops them consistently to an 8:5 frame. Filenames match the design IDs in `resources/js/landing/designCatalog.js`.

These are static previews; opening a card loads the interactive Vue page. Refresh the corresponding image when its design materially changes.

The Event Horizon Blue and Supernova Blue thumbnails were captured after their separate blue editions were reviewed at desktop and phone widths. Both retain their original scene geometry and show the final blue prices and effects.

The two Event Horizon previews were refreshed again for the ShoeGPT robot centerpiece. Their cache version is `guardian`; other previews retain `signature-polish`. The robot cutout and verification are documented in `docs/GUARDIAN-CENTERPIECE.md`.
